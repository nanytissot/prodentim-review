The overlooked finding

Teeth can survive for centuries outside the mouth. Inside it, they struggle.

Fossilized teeth hold up for thousands of years. Yet inside a living mouth, the same enamel can break down within decades — even with regular brushing. Researchers looking into this gap have zeroed in on one overlooked variable: the community of bacteria that lives on your teeth and gums, and whether it's in balance.

A 2021 piece in Nature summarized a growing body of work suggesting that dental problems aren't just about "bad" bacteria attacking your teeth — they're often about a mouth that has too few of the good strains needed to keep things in check. Some common toothpaste and mouthwash ingredients, ironically, can strip out that good bacteria along with the bad.
